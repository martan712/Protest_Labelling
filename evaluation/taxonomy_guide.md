# Protest topic guide — taxonomy version 21-class-v1

Annotate the expressed grievance, not the participant, venue, or tactic. Use the most
specific supported topic. An event may have independently reviewed alternatives, but an
alternative is never inferred from a keyword or from the accepted-pair scoring table.

| Class | Use when the grievance is about |
| --- | --- |
| animal welfare | treatment, protection, or extinction of animals and wildlife |
| blm | Black lives, anti-Black racism, or Black Lives Matter |
| climate | climate change, fossil fuels, emissions, or climate policy |
| culture | tourism, cultural institutions, or the cultural sector |
| discrimination | discrimination or racism when no more specific identity class applies |
| education | schools, teachers, universities, academic conditions, or student education |
| environment | local environmental damage, biodiversity, pollution, or land use not primarily climate |
| farmers | farming, agricultural policy, or farmers' livelihoods |
| health care | hospitals, clinics, medical care, or access to health services |
| housing | housing access, eviction, rent, or residential development |
| immigration | migration policy, migrants, deportation, or detention |
| labor rights | pay, contracts, dismissal, pensions, working conditions, workload, or labor rights |
| lgbtq | LGBTQ+ rights, homophobia, transphobia, or biphobia |
| other | no existing class is supported; record an `other_reason` |
| palestine-israel conflict | a grievance about Israel/Palestine, Gaza, Hamas, or Palestinians |
| pandemic | COVID-19, coronavirus, or pandemic measures |
| policies & politics | general government policy, elections, political opposition, or political rights |
| public services | transport, roads, mobility, municipal services, or other public provision not health, education, or housing |
| ukraine-russia war | the Russia–Ukraine war, military/peace demands tied to it, or its direct effects |
| unjust law enforcement | police misconduct, brutality, repression, or criminalization of protest |
| women rights | women's rights, abortion, sexual violence/harassment, or gender equality |

Examples that prevent participant/venue shortcuts: police demanding pay is **labor rights**;
students protesting coal are **climate** or **environment** according to the grievance;
residents demanding a hospital reopen are **health care**.

Use `other_reason=outside_taxonomy` for a clearly expressed grievance outside this taxonomy,
such as an unrelated disarmament event. Use `other_reason=insufficient_information` when
the event is explicitly a protest but its motive is not reported. `unknown` means only that
no keyword matched; it is not automatically `other`.

Direct accepted scoring pairs are only: climate–environment; unjust law enforcement–blm;
public services–health care/education/housing; and discrimination–women rights/lgbtq/blm.
Pairs are symmetric but not transitive: education–housing and women rights–lgbtq remain wrong.
